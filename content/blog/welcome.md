Welcome to my blog. This is where I will share longer updates about projects, creative work, and topics I am exploring.

This blog takes in content as Markdown files; I don't expect myself to do anything fancy here

If I do decide to do something fancy, I'll probably put a PDF here or something

You can skip the below section; it's mostly for myself in case I forget

New articles can be added by creating another Markdown file in `content/blog`. The filename becomes the article URL, while the title, date, and summary at the top of the file control how it appears on the website.
